04 — Questions

Asked often.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe your business, edit anything in plain language, and publish. If you do code, you can export everything.

Who gets the money from sales?

You do. Payments run through your own Stripe account and are paid out to your bank. Monolith only charges the plan price.

Will the 3D slow my site down?

Scenes are sized to the device. Phones get fewer particles, and visitors who prefer reduced motion see a still frame.

Can I sell sites I make to clients?

Yes, on Studio. Remove our branding, hand the site to the client, and charge whatever you like.
